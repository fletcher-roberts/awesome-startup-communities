Best Founder networks & peer groups Communities for Founders (2026)

20 of the best founder networks & peer groups communities for startup founders in 2026, with who each is best for, format, cost and region. Part of the Awesome Startup Communities directory of 154 communities.

Y Combinator Startup School: YC's free online program with founder curriculum, weekly progress tracking and access to YC co-founder matching.
Best for: First-time founders, idea to pre-seed · Format: Online program + community · Cost: Free · Region: Global
Startup Grind: Global founder community with chapters in hundreds of cities, fireside chats and an annual global conference.
Best for: Meeting your local founder scene in person · Format: Events + chapters · Cost: Free · Region: Global
Venture Café: Free weekly Thursday Gatherings for founders, investors and operators in innovation hubs across several countries.
Best for: Weekly open innovation gatherings · Format: Events + chapters · Cost: Free · Region: Global
1 Million Cups: Weekly local events where two founders present and get candid audience feedback; run in dozens of US cities.
Best for: Early founders wanting feedback from their local community · Format: Events + chapters · Cost: Free · Region: Global
Hampton: Vetted community built around small monthly in-person Core Groups of founders, plus dinners, retreats and an online community. Published pricing (Nov 2026): $2,500 initiation fee and $9,500 annual dues.
Best for: Founders and CEOs of $3M+ revenue companies · Format: Curated peer groups · Cost: Paid (premium, by application) · Region: US, Canada & London (in person)
Founders Network: Peer mentorship community for tech founders with forums, roundtables and city chapters. Published pricing (Nov 2026): roughly $75 to $249 a month, billed annually.
Best for: Full-time tech founders from pre-revenue up · Format: Peer mentorship community · Cost: Paid (by application) · Region: Global (online + chapters)
Entrepreneurs' Organization (EO): Global peer network of business owners organised into small confidential forums and local chapters.
Best for: Owners of businesses past $1M revenue · Format: Peer forums (chapters) · Cost: Paid (revenue threshold) · Region: Global
YPO: Global leadership community for chief executives, organised into chapters and confidential forums.
Best for: Chief executives of larger companies · Format: Peer forums (chapters) · Cost: Paid (revenue threshold) · Region: Global
Vistage: Peer advisory groups led by a trained chair, paired with one-on-one executive coaching.
Best for: CEOs wanting chair-facilitated peer advisory · Format: Peer forums (chapters) · Cost: Paid (revenue threshold) · Region: Global
Global Entrepreneurship Network: Network of entrepreneurship programs in many countries, known for Global Entrepreneurship Week.
Best for: Founders and ecosystem builders in emerging ecosystems · Format: Network + events · Cost: Free · Region: Global
Startup Weekend: Techstars-run weekend events in cities worldwide where people pitch ideas, form teams and build.
Best for: Testing an idea and meeting co-founders in 54 hours · Format: Network + events · Cost: Free · Region: Global
SCORE: US nonprofit offering free business mentoring from experienced volunteers, plus workshops.
Best for: US small business founders wanting free mentors · Format: Network + events · Cost: Free · Region: Global
TiE (The Indus Entrepreneurs): Global non-profit founder network with chapters in many countries, mentoring, events and the TiE Global Summit.
Best for: Founders wanting a mentor and charter-member network · Format: Founder community + events · Cost: Free to join · Region: Global
On Deck (ODF): Founder fellowship built around an in-person week in San Francisco, followed by lifelong access to the alumni community. No equity taken.
Best for: Builders going from idea to conviction · Format: Fellowship / community · Cost: Application · Region: Global
South Park Commons: Community and fund for technologists in the 'minus one to zero' phase, with a member residency and a funded Founder Fellowship.
Best for: Technical founders exploring ideas before company formation · Format: Fellowship / community · Cost: Application · Region: Global
Interact: Fellowship and community of young builders and technologists, with retreats and a lifelong network.
Best for: Young technologists who want a mission-driven peer group · Format: Fellowship / community · Cost: Application · Region: Global
GrowthMentor: Mentorship marketplace and community where founders book calls with vetted operators and marketers.
Best for: Booking 1:1 calls with vetted startup and growth mentors · Format: Peer communities (online) · Cost: Free + paid · Region: Global
Dynamite Circle: Paid, vetted community for remote founders, with an online forum, city meetups and annual events.
Best for: Location-independent founders running profitable companies · Format: Peer communities (online) · Cost: Free + paid · Region: Global
Nomad List: Paid community for digital nomads, with a large share of remote founders and indie makers, plus meetups and chat.
Best for: Remote founders and solo operators who travel · Format: Peer communities (online) · Cost: Free + paid · Region: Global
Chief: Private membership network for women executives built around small peer groups and events.
Best for: Senior women executives · Format: Peer communities (online) · Cost: Free + paid · Region: Global
