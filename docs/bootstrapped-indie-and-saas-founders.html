Best Bootstrapped, indie & SaaS founders Communities for Founders (2026)

7 of the best bootstrapped, indie & saas founders communities for startup founders in 2026, with who each is best for, format, cost and region. Part of the Awesome Startup Communities directory of 154 communities.

Indie Hackers: Long-running forum and interview archive for founders building profitable online businesses.
Best for: Bootstrapped founders sharing revenue and lessons · Format: Forum · Cost: Free · Region: Global (online)
MicroConf Connect: Vetted private community of self-funded SaaS founders, plus mastermind matching and MicroConf conferences.
Best for: Bootstrapped B2B SaaS founders · Format: Private community + conferences · Cost: Paid ($49/mo or $499/yr as of Nov 2026) · Region: Global
TinySeed: Year-long remote accelerator for bootstrapped SaaS founders, with mentorship and a close founder cohort.
Best for: B2B SaaS founders who want capital without a VC treadmill · Format: Accelerator + community · Cost: Equity · Region: Global
WIP: Paid community of makers who log daily progress, ship products and keep each other accountable.
Best for: Makers who want to build in public and ship daily · Format: Community + database · Cost: Free + paid · Region: Global (online)
Starter Story: Database of founder case studies with a paid membership and community.
Best for: Studying how real small businesses were built · Format: Community + database · Cost: Free + paid · Region: Global (online)
No Code Founders: Weekly newsletter and tools directory for founders building with no-code tools.
Best for: Non-technical founders building with no-code tools · Format: Community + database · Cost: Free + paid · Region: Global (online)
SaaStr: B2B SaaS media brand and conference community, including the large annual SaaStr event.
Best for: B2B SaaS founders and go-to-market leaders · Format: Conference + community · Cost: Paid · Region: Global
