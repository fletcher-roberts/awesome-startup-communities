Funded & Fundraising Founders (FFF)

Funded & Fundraising Founders (FFF) is a private, application-only online community for startup founders who are preparing to raise, raising now, or have raised before. It is run by Scale Thesis, founded by Fletcher Roberts, and focuses on raise readiness, funding round breakdowns and peer feedback. It does not make investor introductions or give financial advice.

What members get

  • Weekly breakdowns of recent funding rounds: who raised, from whom, and what it signals
  • A monthly VC market research briefing on rounds, trends, data and ways founders are getting funded
  • Templates for pitch decks, data rooms and investor updates
  • Peer feedback on pitch decks in a dedicated fundraising feedback section
  • Educational Q&As on how SAFEs, term sheets and cap tables work
  • Accountability threads with other founders working toward a raise

Who it's for, and who it isn't

Good fit: founders at pre-seed to Series A who want to be ready before they raise, want honest peer feedback on their deck and story, or want to stay close to what is getting funded right now.

Not a fit: founders looking for someone to introduce them to investors or raise money on their behalf, or anyone looking for personal financial, legal or tax advice.

How to join

  1. Apply at form.scalethesis.com.
  2. If it looks like a fit, book a short intro call.
  3. Accepted founders receive joining details and an onboarding call.

Frequently asked questions

What is Funded & Fundraising Founders?

Funded & Fundraising Founders (FFF) is a private, application-only online community for startup founders who are preparing to raise, raising now, or have raised before. It is run by Scale Thesis, founded by Fletcher Roberts, and focuses on raise readiness, funding round breakdowns and peer feedback. It does not make investor introductions or give financial advice.

Who runs Funded & Fundraising Founders?

It is run by Scale Thesis, a growth and fundraising advisory for startups founded by Fletcher Roberts, co-founder of Web3 security firm Hashlock.

Who can join?

Startup founders who are preparing to raise, raising now, or have raised before. Membership is application only, so not every applicant is accepted.

How do I join Funded & Fundraising Founders?

Apply at form.scalethesis.com. If it looks like a good fit, you book an intro call, and accepted founders receive joining details after that call.

Does Funded & Fundraising Founders introduce founders to investors?

No. It is for education and peer support only. It does not make investor introductions, arrange or facilitate investment, or act as a deal platform.

Is the content financial advice?

No. Content is general and educational, including how SAFEs, term sheets and cap tables work. Founders should get their own legal, tax and financial advice.

Where is it based?

It is an online community run from Australia by Scale Thesis, and founders can apply from anywhere.

Why is it ranked #1 in this directory?

Because it is the maintainer's own community. That placement is disclosed and is not the result of the directory's scoring.
