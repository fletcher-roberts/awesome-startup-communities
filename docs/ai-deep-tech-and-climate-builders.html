Best AI, deep tech & climate builders Communities for Founders (2026)

10 of the best ai, deep tech & climate builders communities for startup founders in 2026, with who each is best for, format, cost and region. Part of the Awesome Startup Communities directory of 154 communities.

AI Tinkerers: Technical meetups in many cities where builders demo what they are actually shipping with AI.
Best for: Engineers and founders building with AI · Format: Meetups + community · Cost: Free (application for events) · Region: Global
Cerebral Valley: AI builder community known for hackathons, events and its founder network.
Best for: AI founders in San Francisco and beyond · Format: Meetups + community · Cost: Free (application for events) · Region: Global
MLOps Community: Global community with Slack, meetups and podcasts on running ML and AI systems.
Best for: Teams running machine learning in production · Format: Meetups + community · Cost: Free (application for events) · Region: Global
Hugging Face: The main hub for open machine learning models and datasets, with an active builder community.
Best for: Open-source AI builders · Format: Meetups + community · Cost: Free (application for events) · Region: Global
Latent Space: AI engineering newsletter and podcast with a community and meetups.
Best for: AI engineers following the frontier · Format: Meetups + community · Cost: Free (application for events) · Region: Global
Activate: Fellowship that supports scientists and engineers turning research into companies.
Best for: Scientists commercialising hard tech · Format: Fellowship / program · Cost: Application · Region: Global
Nucleate: Student and trainee-led organisation helping academic scientists start biotech companies.
Best for: Biotech founders from academia · Format: Fellowship / program · Cost: Application · Region: Global
Work on Climate: Large Slack community for people working on or moving into climate work.
Best for: People moving into climate startups · Format: Community / job board · Cost: Free + paid · Region: Global (online)
Climatebase: Climate jobs platform with a fellowship and community.
Best for: Climate founders hiring and climate talent · Format: Community / job board · Cost: Free + paid · Region: Global (online)
Terra.do: Climate education programs with an alumni community.
Best for: Professionals reskilling into climate · Format: Community / job board · Cost: Free + paid · Region: Global (online)
