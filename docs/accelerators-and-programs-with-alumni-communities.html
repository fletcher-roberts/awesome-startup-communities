Best Accelerators & programs with alumni communities Communities for Founders (2026)

13 of the best accelerators & programs with alumni communities communities for startup founders in 2026, with who each is best for, format, cost and region. Part of the Awesome Startup Communities directory of 154 communities.

Y Combinator: The best-known startup accelerator; alumni get access to Bookface, its private founder network.
Best for: Ambitious founders wanting the strongest alumni network · Format: Accelerator · Cost: Equity · Region: Global (SF-based)
Techstars: Global accelerator network with programs in many cities and a large alumni and mentor community.
Best for: Founders wanting a mentor-driven accelerator · Format: Accelerator · Cost: Equity · Region: Global
500 Global: Global venture firm and accelerator with programs and alumni across dozens of countries.
Best for: Founders in emerging and global markets · Format: Accelerator · Cost: Equity · Region: Global
Antler: Early-stage investor that runs residencies to help individuals meet co-founders and start companies.
Best for: Pre-team founders wanting a co-founder and day-zero funding · Format: Accelerator · Cost: Equity · Region: Global
Entrepreneurs First: Talent investor that assembles cohorts of individuals who form teams and build companies together.
Best for: Talented individuals starting a company before having a team · Format: Accelerator · Cost: Equity · Region: Global
Founder Institute: Pre-seed accelerator running part-time programs in many cities worldwide, with a large global alumni network.
Best for: Idea-stage founders in cities outside major hubs · Format: Accelerator · Cost: Equity · Region: Global
Plug and Play: Innovation platform connecting startups with corporations through industry accelerators worldwide.
Best for: Startups wanting corporate partners and pilots · Format: Accelerator · Cost: Equity · Region: Global
Thiel Fellowship: Two-year grant program for young people who want to build new things, with a tight fellow network.
Best for: Young founders who want to build instead of study · Format: Fellowship / grant · Cost: Non-dilutive or small cheque · Region: Global
Z Fellows: One-week fellowship that gives early builders a small investment and access to founders and investors.
Best for: Builders who want a quick, small cheque and a network · Format: Fellowship / grant · Cost: Non-dilutive or small cheque · Region: Global
Emergent Ventures: Fast grants and fellowships for people with high-impact ideas, administered by the Mercatus Center.
Best for: Ambitious individuals with unusual projects · Format: Fellowship / grant · Cost: Non-dilutive or small cheque · Region: Global
Neo: Mentorship community, scholars program and accelerator for exceptional engineers and founders.
Best for: Top technical students and young founders · Format: Fellowship / grant · Cost: Non-dilutive or small cheque · Region: Global
Seedstars: Investor and program operator backing startups in emerging markets, with a global founder network.
Best for: Founders in emerging markets · Format: Accelerator / investor · Cost: Equity · Region: Emerging markets
Endeavor: Global community of high-impact scale-up entrepreneurs, selected and supported by mentors and peers.
Best for: Scale-up founders in growth markets · Format: Endeavor network · Cost: Selection-based · Region: Global
