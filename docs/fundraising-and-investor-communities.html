Best Fundraising & investor communities Communities for Founders (2026)

8 of the best fundraising & investor communities communities for startup founders in 2026, with who each is best for, format, cost and region. Part of the Awesome Startup Communities directory of 154 communities.

Funded & Fundraising Founders (maintainer's community): Private peer community run by Scale Thesis for founders before, during and after a raise. Members get weekly breakdowns of recent funding rounds, a monthly VC market research briefing, deck/data room/investor update templates, peer pitch-deck feedback, educational Q&As on how SAFEs, term sheets and cap tables work, and accountability threads. It is for education and peer support only: it does not make investor introductions, arrange or facilitate investment, or give financial, legal or tax advice.
Best for: Founders preparing to raise, raising now, or who have raised before · Format: Private online community (by application) · Cost: Application only · Region: Global (online)
AngelList: Investing platform and network for startups, syndicates and angels, with tools for fundraising and fund admin.
Best for: Founders and angels using syndicates and rolling funds · Format: Investor network / platform · Cost: Free to join · Region: Global
Hustle Fund Angel Squad: Community and education program from Hustle Fund for new and experienced angels, with deal flow from the fund.
Best for: Founders and operators who want to learn angel investing · Format: Angel investing community · Cost: Paid · Region: Global (online)
Keiretsu Forum: Global angel network with regional chapters that hear startup pitches and invest together.
Best for: Founders pitching to organised angel groups · Format: Angel network · Cost: Membership (investors) · Region: US + global chapters
All Raise: Nonprofit working to accelerate women in venture, with founder programs, office hours and a funders network.
Best for: Women founders and funders · Format: Community + nonprofit · Cost: Free · Region: US
Sydney Angels: Sydney-based angel group that runs regular pitch sessions for early-stage Australian startups.
Best for: Australian founders raising from angels · Format: Angel network · Cost: Membership (investors) · Region: Australia
Melbourne Angels: Melbourne angel investor group that reviews and invests in early-stage startups.
Best for: Victorian founders raising seed rounds · Format: Angel network · Cost: Membership (investors) · Region: Australia
Scale Investors: Angel network that invests in female-led, high-growth Australian startups and trains new investors.
Best for: Female-founded Australian startups raising capital · Format: Angel network · Cost: Membership (investors) · Region: Australia
